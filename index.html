Family owned

Lake people, serving lake people.

Shane and Lisa Reffett started Rochester Boat Company in 2009 after struggling to get their own lift and dock installed. What began as pier, dock and lift work grew one neighbor's request at a time into a full-service marine dealer.

Today we sell boats, motors, lifts and docks, then service, detail, install, remove and store them. When you call, you get people who live on the water and answer to their neighbors.

— Shane & Lisa Reffett, Owners
